# ContributorOps Product Overview

## Product vision

ContributorOps helps developers turn real open-source contributions into job-ready proof of work. The product is designed for people who want stronger public evidence than generic side projects, resume claims, or private work alone.

## Target users

- Developers targeting API, backend, Angular, platform, or developer-advocate roles
- Career switchers who need public proof of execution
- Bootcamps or cohorts that want structured open-source contribution workflows
- Teams that want to coordinate contribution sourcing and reporting without losing maintainer trust

## Core workflows

1. Discover high-quality issues
2. Score issues transparently
3. Generate a daily contribution plan
4. Prepare contribution notes, maintainer questions, and PR drafts
5. Track progress in a portfolio workflow
6. Convert the work into public proof, resume bullets, LinkedIn posts, and interview stories

## Why proof-of-work matters

Hiring outcomes improve when developers can point to real shipped work in real repositories. ContributorOps is built to make that work more intentional, more visible, and easier to explain in interviews or recruiter conversations.

## Important boundary

ContributorOps is not positioned as a spam bot, fake contribution farming tool, or deceptive automation product. It is designed around human approval, issue relevance, rate limits, and maintainer trust.
